10-step AI algorithm

  1. Collect order, inventory, lens-lab, store-transfer, staffing, carrier and complaint data.
  2. Validate IDs, timestamps, prescription status and stock records.
  3. Build the complete order timeline.
  4. Calculate dwell time, lateness, transfers, rework and failed attempts.
  5. Compare similar orders by product type, location, carrier, shift and promise.
  6. Detect verification holds, lab queues, stock mismatch and dispatch delays.
  7. Rank likely causes and show supporting evidence.
  8. Simulate source allocation, staffing, dispatch and route changes.
  9. Recommend actions, owners and expected KPI outcomes.
  10. Measure impact, run pilots and recalibrate.

How to interpret

Find the order stage with the largest avoidable dwell time. Trace prescription verification, stock allocation, lens processing and quality checks where applicable, picking/packing, transfer, courier handover and last mile.

Normalize by order volume and compare similar cohorts. The AI should distinguish correlation from causation and show timestamps behind each recommendation.

This is a standalone front-end demo. Live use requires authenticated APIs and a secure backend.
