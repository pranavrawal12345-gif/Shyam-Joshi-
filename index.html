Concise AI Executive Conclusion: Why High Leads Did Not Drive Revenue

1. The Root Cause of Funnel Leakage: High lead volumes did not translate into proportional revenue growth because marketing shifted budget toward low-intent top-of-funnel acquisition (Meta Ads and broad display), where lead qualification rates collapsed to 9.9%. Over 90% of these leads were students, micro-SMBs, or accidental clicks with zero budget authority.

2. Sales Pipeline Congestion: Sales SDR capacity became choked qualifying low-quality leads, causing sales velocity to decelerate and delaying follow-up on high-value inbound enterprise prospects from Google Search and LinkedIn.

3. Average Deal Value Dilution: The customers who did convert from low-intent channels purchased the smallest SMB packages ($3.8k - $4.8k ACV), while high-intent enterprise channels (LinkedIn ABM and Referral) generated deals averaging $28k - $32k ACV.

4. Immediate Strategic Remedy: Immediately reallocate $50,000+ of marketing spend from Meta Ads to LinkedIn ABM and Google High-Intent Search, eliminate top-of-funnel "CPL" incentives, and institute strict ICP gatekeeping (domain & company size verification) before leads are routed to sales reps.
